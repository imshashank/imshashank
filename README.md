# Shashank Agarwal

**Founder & CEO of [Noveum AI](https://noveum.ai) · Creator of [API.market](https://api.market) · Author of [Intelligence at Scale](https://intelligenceatscale.ai)**

I build AI infrastructure and products for developers. My work connects production machine learning, AI agent evaluation, and the APIs agents use to act.

## What I’m building

- **[Noveum AI](https://noveum.ai/en)** helps teams trace and evaluate production AI agents, investigate failures, and review evidence for improvements. The work spans observability, evaluation, synthetic testing, and engineering workflows.
- **[API.market](https://api.market)** is a marketplace for APIs and AI models. It helps developers discover services and helps providers publish and monetize them. Its [MCP gateway](https://api.market/mcp) connects compatible AI clients to marketplace tools.

## Background

I worked on the original AWS SageMaker launch team, including hyperparameter optimization, and contributed to the AWS SDK for PHP. I later worked on machine-learning-powered personalization at Amazon Prime Video. My experience also includes engineering leadership at Activeloop and Levity, and founding Hopdata and PipFeed.

I started publishing hacking tutorials on HackItEasy while I was in school, and later built Dailyjag, a media business. Building software and explaining what I learn have stayed connected throughout my career.

I hold a master’s degree in computer science from The Ohio State University and was a Forbes Technology Council member from 2021 to 2024. [Career and project history →](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shashankagarwal2/)
